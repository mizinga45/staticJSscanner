Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay?

Send payment via M-Pesa to +255 712 345 678 (SecScan JS). Use your username as the reference. Your plan will be activated within 24 hours after confirmation.

Yes! The Free plan gives you 3 full scans — no credit card, no commitment. Upgrade anytime when you need more capacity.

What vulnerability types are detected?

SQL Injection, XSS, Command Injection, eval() injection, Hardcoded Secrets, Path Traversal, SSRF, ReDoS, Insecure Randomness, Prototype Pollution, Open Redirect, and more.

Yes, simply stop payment. Your plan remains active until the end of the billing period. Contact support to request a refund within 7 days.
